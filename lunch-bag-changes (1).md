# Lunch Bag Website — Client Change Requests

Site: https://lunch-bag-one.vercel.app/
Reference (style/banners): https://www.idlizz.com
Context: Hyderabad-based office lunch delivery, **North + South Indian combo meals**.

Apply the changes below in the given order.

---

## 1. Branding

1. **Color theme**: Change the whole site color palette to match the **logo colors**. Extract the colors from the logo and use them for buttons, headers, accents, badges, and backgrounds.
   - **Do NOT use a red + black combination.**
2. **Logo**: Make the logo **bigger and clearer** (header, footer, and hero). Use a high-resolution version, no blur, enough padding around it.

## 2. Pricing

1. Show **Regular price: ₹219 per meal** (strike-through).
2. Show **Special price: ₹179 per meal** if ordered via **WhatsApp or the website**.
3. Display this consistently on the hero, menu, and order sections.

## 3. Menu (single menu)

1. Keep **one single menu** (no separate day-wise menu pages).
2. Add a note: *"Veg curry and dal options vary on different days."*
3. Each meal includes:
   - Steamed Rice
   - Chapati / Phulka / Lachha Paratha
   - Dal: Palak Kura Pappu / Gongura Pappu / Chukkakura Pappu / Bachali Kura Pappu / Dal Fry
   - Dry Veg Curry (South Indian / North Indian style)
   - Wet Veg Curry (South Indian / North Indian style)
   - Amul Curd / Raita / Curd Rice
   - Chutney / Pachadi
   - Fryums
   - Sweet (Gulab Jamun / Rasgulla)
   - Pickle sachet
   - Salt sachet
   - Bisleri water bottle (200 ml)
   - Spoon / fork & napkin set
   - Mouth freshener sachet

## 8. Content Changes

1. **Our Story**: Rewrite to be **more interesting and engaging** (a short story-style narrative: why we started, home-style North + South food for office-goers, daily freshness, the people behind it).
   - **Remove any mention of NCR.** Hyderabad is in South India; position the brand as Hyderabad-based.
2. **Hygiene line**: Remove the phrase **"hospital grade hygiene"**. Replace with something else, e.g. *"Prepared fresh in a clean, hygienic kitchen with strict quality checks"* (confirm final wording with client).

## 9. Images

1. Use **good quality, real food images** (high-resolution, appetizing).
2. **Add South Indian meal photos** as well (rice, pappu, curries, curd rice, chutney) since we serve North + South combos.
3. Add **banner-style images** like on idlizz.com (hero banner, offer banner, pass banner, delivery banner).

## 10. App / Account Access (Meal Pass Users)

Provide direct app/web account access where users can:
- **Control their meal days** (choose which days they want food)

- **Maintain their wallet** (balance, deductions, recharge, history) when opting for meal passes

Follow the look, banner layout, and overall feel of **www.idlizz.com** (the client's South Indian breakfast website).
